Highlight clips from your own gameplay

Auto Clipper is a personal desktop app for Windows that runs on the creator's own PC. It finds the best moments (multi-kills, wallbangs, smoke kills and similar) in Counter-Strike 2 gameplay recordings the creator made themselves, and turns each one into a short vertical video.

How it works

  1. The app scans the creator's own replay recordings on their PC.
  2. Each highlight is cut and edited into a 9:16 clip, saved on the same PC.
  3. The creator reviews every clip in the app.
  4. Only when the creator clicks Post is that clip uploaded to the creator's own account on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, with the caption they chose.

TikTok

The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit). When they click Post, the clip is sent to their TikTok inbox, where they finish and publish it in the TikTok app, or, if they choose direct posting, it is posted with the caption and privacy setting they picked. The app never posts on its own, never reads other people's content, and only uses the creator's display name to show which account is connected.

Auto Clipper is not a hosted service: there are no user accounts, no servers and no data collection. Everything runs and is stored on the creator's own computer.
