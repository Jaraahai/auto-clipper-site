Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

Auto Clipper runs entirely on your own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. The author never receives your videos, your account data or your login tokens.

What the app uses

  • Your gameplay recordings, read from folders you choose on your PC, to find highlights and make clips. Clips are saved on your PC.
  • Access tokens for the accounts you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook). They are stored only in the app's data folder on your PC and are used only to upload the clips you choose to post.
  • From TikTok: your basic profile info (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic), only to show which account is connected; and permission to upload videos to your inbox or post them (video.upload, video.publish), used only when you click Post on a clip.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or send your data to anyone other than the platform you post to.
  • It does not read your followers, messages, other videos or anyone else's content.
  • It does not post anything without you clicking Post.

Deleting your data

Click Disconnect in the app to delete a platform's token from your PC, or uninstall the app and delete its data folder to remove everything. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google or Meta account settings. Videos you already posted stay on those platforms until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13.
